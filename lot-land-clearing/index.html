Common Jobs

What Lot & Land Clearing Covers

These are the most common situations where homeowners, builders, and landowners call Barton's for clearing work.

New Construction & Building Prep

A wooded or overgrown lot needs to be clear before construction can start. Barton's fells and removes trees, grinds stumps, and chips brush so the site is fully cleared and ready for a builder or contractor to move in.

Brush Removal & Overgrowth Cleanup

Properties that haven't been maintained accumulate years of brush, vines, and scrub growth that crowd out usable space. Clearing that overgrowth restores the property's footprint and makes the land genuinely usable again.

Lot Sale & Development Prep

A cleared, presentable lot shows better and often sells faster. Whether you're listing land or preparing it for development, clearing the property gives buyers or developers a clear picture of what they're working with.

Wood Chipping & On-Site Processing

Clearing generates a significant volume of material. Barton's chips brush and small-diameter wood on site, dramatically reducing the debris volume. Chips can be spread on the property or hauled off — the choice is yours.

The Process

How a Clearing Job Gets Done

Every clearing job is different, but the approach is consistent: assess the site, work it systematically, and leave it clean when we're done.

1

Site Walk & Assessment

Before any equipment moves in, we walk the property to understand the scope — tree sizes, brush density, access points, and what you need the finished site to look like. Stump grinding, debris haul-off, and any specialty work are scoped at the same visit so there are no surprises.

2

Clearing, Felling & Chipping

Trees are felled in a controlled sequence that keeps the work area safe and efficient. The chipper runs alongside, processing brush and limbs as the crew works through the property. Large wood sections are bucked and staged for haul-off. The stump grinder comes in after the trees are down.

3

Final Cleanup & Debris Removal

Once the clearing work is complete, all debris — chips, logs, cut material — is either hauled off-site or spread as directed. We don't leave a clearing job looking like a demolition site. The property goes from overgrown to clear in a single mobilization.

Full-Scale Clearing Operation

Equipment & Crew for the Full Job

A lot or land clearing job isn't one piece of equipment — it's the whole operation working together. Barton's brings the chip truck, the chipper, the stump grinder, and the crew to handle it all in a single mobilization. Trees come down, brush goes into the chipper, stumps get ground below grade, and the site gets cleaned up before we leave.

For larger properties or trees with limited drop zones, crane-assisted removal is part of the Barton's capability — the same crew, the same job, with additional equipment when the site calls for it. After the clearing is done, any remaining stumps can be handled with stump grinding in the same visit.
