Privacy Policy

Simple and straightforward — what we collect, what we do with it, and how to reach us with questions.

Last updated: July 2026

Who We Are

Barton's Tree Care is a licensed tree service company based in Crofton, Maryland. We serve homeowners throughout Maryland and have since 1989.

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us at info@bartonstreecare.com or by calling (410) 710-0164.

What We Collect

The only way we collect personal information on this website is through our contact form. When you submit a request for a quote, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The type of service you're interested in
  • Any message or details you choose to include

This information is submitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, and delivered to us by email. We use it only to respond to your inquiry.

We do not collect any information if you simply browse the site without submitting the form.

What We Do With It

We use your contact information to get back to you about your tree service request. That's it.

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it. We do not share it with any marketing companies or third parties for their own use. The only parties who handle your submission are us and Web3Forms (which processes and forwards the form data to us).

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site — which pages get traffic, how long people stay, and where they come from. This data is anonymous and does not include your name, email, or phone number.

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using Google's browser add-on.

We don't use any other tracking tools. No Facebook Pixel, no retargeting, no session recordings.

Cookies

We don't set any cookies ourselves. Google Analytics sets a small cookie to track visits anonymously. No login, no accounts, and no persistent data is stored by Barton's Tree Care directly.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of external services:

  • Web3Forms — processes contact form submissions
  • Google Analytics — anonymous site usage data
  • Google Fonts — loads the fonts used on this site
  • Google Maps — shows our service area in the footer

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We only use them to make the site work and to respond to inquiries.

Your Information

If you submitted a quote request and want us to delete your information, just email us at info@bartonstreecare.com and we'll take care of it.

We don't store form submissions in a database — they come to us as emails — so once we delete the email on our end, it's gone.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how we handle your information in a meaningful way, we'll update this page. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.

Questions? Email us at info@bartonstreecare.com.
